ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

BloodCare: E-Blood Bank Management

BloodCare is a web-based E-Blood Bank Management System developed to facilitate and organize blood donation activities through a centralized digital platform.

The system enables users to register as blood donors, search for registered donors, and submit blood requests by providing the necessary patient and hospital details. It also includes administrative functionality to support the organized management of donor records and blood requests.

WHY IT MATTERS

Why Blood Donation Matters

Critical Need

Blood is needed for emergencies, surgeries, childbirth complications and medical treatments.

Supports Patients

Blood donations help patients with thalassemia, anaemia and other medical conditions that require blood transfusions.

Emergency Availability

Regular blood donations help blood banks maintain an adequate supply for accidents, emergency surgeries and other urgent medical needs.

Save a Life

Your voluntary donation can give patients and their families hope during difficult times.

Donor Age & Eligibility Criteria

Blood donation is a simple way to help people in need. Before donating, make sure you meet the basic eligibility requirements. Final eligibility is decided by qualified blood-bank staff.

  • Age Requirement: Meet the minimum age requirement set by local blood-donation guidelines.
  • Weight Requirement: Meet the minimum weight criteria required for safe donation.
  • Good Health: Be feeling well on the day of donation and inform staff about any illness or medication.
  • Medical Screening: Complete the health questions and screening conducted by blood-bank staff.
  • Valid Identification: Carry valid identification if required by the blood bank.
  • Before Donation: Eat a proper meal, drink enough water and follow the staff's instructions.
